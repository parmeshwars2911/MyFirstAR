# Image credits — MH Grade 6 decks

Reference images used in the "The Universe" deck are **public-domain NASA
imagery**, included as accurate reference for recreation by the graphic
designer. All physics schematics in the other decks (simple machines, work &
energy, sound, light & shadows, magnets) are original vector diagrams
generated in this repo (`tools/diagrams_mh06.py` and reused primitives).
